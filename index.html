How the bias check works

Outlet: each outlet's overall leaning, roughly in line with public media-bias charts such as AllSides and Ad Fontes Media.

Wording: the story is scanned for phrases one side tends to use more (undocumented immigrants, gun safety … illegal aliens, gun rights …) and for loaded words like “slams” or “bombshell”.

Slant: one number from −100 (left) to +100 (right). The outlet counts for most of it (Far Left −100, Left −67, Center Left −33, Center 0, Center Right +33, Right +67, Far Right +100); the wording counts more the more buzzwords a story has, up to 60% of the score. Anything within 15 of zero shows as Center.

It is a word count, not a judgment of accuracy or fairness. Neutral wording can still leave things out, and one phrase can tip a short headline.
